How it works

In with a card. Out with a tap on your phone.

Two entry lanes and two exit lanes keep traffic moving. Here is how a visit works.

1Coming in

  1. Slow down at the entry dispenser

    Follow the green Entry Only sign and stop gently beside the machine.

  2. Press the button and take a card

    The barrier opens and you can drive in.

  3. Keep your card and park as you like

    Choose any bay and keep to 5 km/h. You will need the card to leave.

2Pay from your phone

*215*card no.#

Amount due: $1.00

1. Accept

2. Cancel

Enter PIN ••••

  1. Before you drive to the exit, dial *215*, your card number and #.
  2. You will see the amount for the time you parked. Accept to pay.
  3. Enter your PIN. Once the payment succeeds you are ready to exit.

3Leaving

  1. Slow down at the exit dispenser

    Follow the orange Exit Only sign and insert your card.

  2. The barrier opens. Drive out.

    Safe travels. See you next time.

Any problem at the exit?

Stop at the barrier. Our team will sort it out on the spot, or come to our office as you leave.
